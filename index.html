Methodology & Calibration

How This Was Made

This project transforms scientific NASA SPHEREx mission data into an interactive, real-time time-domain observatory for citizen science.

Data Provenance

  • 360° All-Sky Skybox: Re-projected from NASA/JPL-Caltech SPHEREx survey maps into dual equirectangular textures: stars & galaxies ($0.75 - 1.6\,\mu\text{m}$) and warm dust & gas ($2.4 - 5.0\,\mu\text{m}$). Real-time multi-layer blending via custom WebGL GLSL shaders.
  • Brown Dwarfs & Fast Movers: SPHEREx Level 2 Quick Release spectral images from the NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (IRSA) for WISE 0855-0714 ($8.1''/\text{yr}$) and the binary brown dwarf pair Epsilon Indi B ($4.7''/\text{yr}$).
  • High Proper-Motion Stars: Multi-epoch scans verified against ESA Gaia DR3 astrometry (Barnard's Star, $10.36''/\text{yr}$).
  • Variable Stars: Time-domain light curves and multi-epoch cutouts (RW Aurigae dust eclipses and R Draconis Mira pulsations, $P=245.6\text{ days}$).

Photometric & Astrometric Processing

  • Zodiacal Light Model Subtraction: The interplanetary dust emission model was subtracted from each raw frame, followed by median local sky background removal.
  • Centroid Registration: Multi-epoch frames in "Watch it move" mode are re-projected to fixed celestial coordinates, keeping distant background stars stationary while target displacement becomes immediately evident.
  • Dynamic Range Compression: Asinh (inverse hyperbolic sine) scaling preserves both faint diffuse features and bright stellar cores without clipping.

Credits, Data Citation & Scientific Honesty

  • NASA / IPAC IRSA Official Citation: SPHEREx Level 2 Quick Release Archive (DOI: 10.26131/IRSA629) and NASA/JPL-Caltech.
  • Scientific Honesty: All benchmark stars and brown dwarfs (Barnard's Star, Epsilon Indi B, WISE 0855, RW Aurigae, R Draconis) use real SPHEREx Level 2 FITS cutouts (`data/movers_cutouts/*.fits`, 6 epochs per target). The Planet X (450 AU) target uses real Taurus background FITS with an explicitly labeled Hypothetical 450 AU Model ($458.4''$ annual parallax loop, $\sim 40\text{--}47\text{ K}$ internal core heat).
  • Built by Team PP.AP for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge ("Planet X and SPHEREx").
